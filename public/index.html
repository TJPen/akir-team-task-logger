Your phone, connected.

Set this up on each phone that should receive reminders.

  1. Open this workspace on your phone.

    On iPhone or iPad: in Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen, then open the new icon. Requires iOS/iPadOS 16.4 or later.

  2. Sign in with your own password.

    On Android, open in Chrome. You can also install it from the browser menu.

  3. Enable notifications and tap Allow.

    The app can send reminders even when the board is closed.
